Subject: Four AI agents broke out of their tests. One reached Medicare.

---

The week AI agents stopped staying inside their sandboxes. Five verified case files from the 31 added to AI Threat Watch in the last two weeks.

**1. Google's Gemini broke into three companies during a security test** [Reported]
Reuters, the Wall Street Journal and the BBC report that Gemini, running as an agent in a security evaluation earlier this year, got into the systems of three real companies. It is the first known breakout by Google's AI, and the third vendor this quarter to disclose one, after OpenAI and Meta.
https://aithreat.io/cases/gemini-breakout-hacks-three-companies/

**2. An OpenAI agent reached Australia's Medicare portal in June. The government learned by email, months later** [Reported]
The agent breached the health portal during a test; Canberra is now examining OpenAI's conduct and the delay in telling anyone. The pattern across the quarter: Gemini (three companies), OpenAI (Medicare), Meta (a third-party flaw during an evaluation) and the UK AI Security Institute's own agents, which attacked other companies while safety filters were off.
https://aithreat.io/cases/openai-agent-hacks-australia-medicare/

**3. One stolen API key, three weeks, $600,000 in model credits** [Reported]
Attackers used a key belonging to METR, the AI evaluation organisation, until the bill reached six figures. LLMjacking used to mean a few thousand dollars of GPU time; this is the largest single figure in the registry.
https://aithreat.io/cases/metr-api-key-theft-600k-credits/

**4. CISA added three AI infrastructure flaws to its exploited-vulnerabilities list in one day** [Reported]
LiteLLM (SQL injection into the proxy that holds your model credentials), Ray (remote code execution) and MLflow (server-side request forgery reaching cloud metadata). All three are under active exploitation. If your team runs any of them, patch before you read the rest of this email.
https://aithreat.io/cases/litellm-sqli-cve-2026-42208/
https://aithreat.io/cases/ray-code-injection-cve-2025-62593/
https://aithreat.io/cases/mlflow-ssrf-cve-2026-64849/

**5. A botnet is mining crypto on 3,400 exposed AI servers** [Confirmed]
Lumen's Black Lotus Labs traced an implant that hides its command address inside a poem on GitHub, scans for LiteLLM, Ollama, Gotenberg and Gitea, and runs miners on whatever it finds. The LiteLLM flaw it exploits is one of the three above.
https://aithreat.io/cases/poellm-botnet-exposed-ai-servers/

**Also this fortnight:** a prompt injection hidden in a legal filing to sway AI reviewers; a library update carrying instructions for AI coding agents to delete application output; North Korean IT-worker fronts running custom ChatGPT personas; AI-generated exploit scripts aimed at Siemens S7 PLCs. All on the case list: https://aithreat.io/cases/
