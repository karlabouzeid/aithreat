Official warnings and preparedness

Public warnings, exercises, advisories and new rules from heads of government, ministers, regulators, central banks, intergovernmental bodies and national cyber agencies. Each entry links to its source.

  • WarningOct 8, 2026Danish Ministry of Culture · MinisterDenmark

    Denmark tables bill to ban sharing realistic deepfakes of people's faces and voices without consent

    Culture Minister Zenia Stampe introduced a copyright amendment banning sharing of realistic deepfakes of a person's face or voice without consent, covering artists' performances, with satire exempt. Intended start is early 2027.

    Source: Euronews

  • WarningAug 17, 2026Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC) · RegulatorAustralia

    ASIC warns scammers use AI to build networks of deepfake websites and endorsements

    ASIC said deepfake videos of celebrities and politicians in investment scams rose sharply, removed over 19,400 scams in FY26 (up 182%), and advised that a simple online search is not enough to verify an offer.

    Source: ASIC

  • AdvisoryJul 20, 2026FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center · Security agencyUnited States

    FBI warns scammers impersonate IC3 using deepfake videos of public figures

    The FBI said recovery scammers use AI-generated deepfake videos of public figures, including a senior FBI leader, to promote fake IC3 complaint sites that harvest personal and financial data from past fraud victims.

    Source: FBI IC3

  • RuleJun 29, 2026Council of the EU and European Parliament · LegislatureEU

    EU adopts AI Act amendments banning AI 'nudifier' systems

    The Digital Omnibus on AI, approved by Parliament on 16 June and the Council on 29 June 2026, bans AI systems that create CSAM or non-consensual intimate depictions of identifiable people, with compliance due by 2 December 2026.

    Source: European Parliament Legislative Train

  • WarningJun 15, 2026Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA) · RegulatorSouth Africa

    South Africa's FSCA warns of deepfake videos of journalists promoting investment scam

    The FSCA warned that AI-generated videos of journalist Maya Fisher-French and broadcaster Bruce Whitfield were used in social media ads promoting a 'Quantum AI' scheme promising unrealistic returns.

    Source: Eyewitness News

  • AdvisoryJun 10, 2026European Commission · IntergovernmentalEU

    European Commission publishes Code of Practice on marking and labelling AI-generated content

    The voluntary code gives generative AI providers and deployers steps to meet AI Act transparency duties applying from 2 August 2026, under which deepfakes must be clearly labelled.

    Source: European Commission

  • RuleMay 19, 2026Federal Trade Commission · RegulatorUnited States

    FTC begins enforcing TAKE IT DOWN Act removal duties for intimate images and deepfakes

    From 19 May 2026 covered platforms must remove non-consensual intimate images, including AI-generated deepfakes, within 48 hours of a valid request. The FTC opened a complaint site and wrote to 15 major platforms.

    Source: Federal Trade Commission

  • RuleFeb 10, 2026Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) · MinisterIndia

    India amends IT Rules to require labelling of AI-generated content and faster takedowns

    The amendment covers synthetically generated information including deepfakes, requiring labels and traceable metadata, and cutting removal timelines for unlawful content from 36 to 3 hours and for intimate-image complaints to 2 hours.

    Source: Press Information Bureau, Government of India

  • WarningFeb 3, 2026Information Commissioner's Office · RegulatorUnited Kingdom

    UK ICO opens formal investigations into X and xAI over Grok sexualised imagery

    The ICO opened investigations into whether X and xAI processed personal data lawfully in developing and deploying Grok, after reports it was used to create non-consensual sexual imagery, including of children.

    Source: Information Commissioner's Office

  • WarningJan 26, 2026European Commission · IntergovernmentalEU

    European Commission opens Digital Services Act investigation into Grok on X

    The Commission opened formal proceedings to assess whether X assessed and mitigated risks from integrating Grok, including manipulated sexually explicit images, saying these risks seem to have materialised and exposed EU citizens to serious harm.

    Source: European Commission

  • WarningJan 12, 2026Ofcom · RegulatorUnited Kingdom

    Ofcom opens Online Safety Act investigation into X over Grok-generated sexualised images

    Ofcom opened a formal investigation into whether X met its Online Safety Act duties after reports that Grok was used to create non-consensual sexualised images of real people, including children. Fines of up to 10% of worldwide revenue are possible.

    Source: CyberScoop
