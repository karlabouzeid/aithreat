Alertas oficiales y preparación

Alertas públicas, simulacros, avisos y nuevas normas de jefes de gobierno, ministros, reguladores, bancos centrales, organismos intergubernamentales y agencias nacionales de ciberseguridad. Cada entrada enlaza con su fuente.

  • Alerta19 oct 2026INTERPOL · IntergubernamentalINT

    INTERPOL launches public awareness campaign on AI deepfake scams

    INTERPOL released three AI-generated videos showing scams in which criminals use deepfakes to pose as relatives or colleagues, including requests for bank transfers, and advised verifying unexpected requests through trusted channels.

    Fuente: INTERPOL

  • Alerta31 ago 2026Financial Stability Board (Chair Andrew Bailey), letter to G20 finance ministers and central bank governors · IntergubernamentalINT

    FSB Chair warns G20 of cyber risks from frontier AI models

    The FSB Chair told G20 finance ministers and central bank governors that frontier AI models pose cyber risks to the financial system and urged authorities to support their safe release and deployment.

    Fuente: Financial Stability Board

  • Aviso31 jul 2026European Supervisory Authorities (EBA, EIOPA and ESMA) · ReguladorEU

    EU supervisory authorities publish joint statement on ICT risks from frontier AI models

    EBA, EIOPA and ESMA called for consistent, risk-based supervision of ICT risks from frontier AI, set out prevention, detection and management measures for financial entities, and updated on DORA oversight of critical ICT third-party providers.

    Fuente: European Banking Authority

  • Aviso7 jul 2026European Central Bank Banking Supervision (Claudia Buch, Chair of the Supervisory Board) · Banco centralEU

    ECB asks significant banks for action plans on AI-enabled cybersecurity threats

    Letter SSM-2026-0301 to CEOs of significant institutions says AI models can find vulnerabilities and generate exploits at unprecedented speed, and asks banks to strengthen ICT resilience and submit an action plan to their supervisory teams.

    Fuente: European Central Bank

  • Norma2 jun 2026Donald Trump, President of the United States (Executive Order 14409) · Jefe de gobiernoEstados Unidos

    US executive order on advanced AI innovation and security addresses AI-enabled cyber threats

    The order directs agencies to prioritise cyber defence of government systems, has Treasury set up a voluntary AI cybersecurity clearinghouse, names rural hospitals, community banks and local utilities, and creates a voluntary pre-release framework for frontier models.

    Fuente: The White House

  • Aviso2 jun 2026Hong Kong Monetary Authority · Banco centralHong Kong

    HKMA circular on strengthening cyber resilience amid AI-empowered cyber threats

    Circular to all authorized institutions said frontier AI models can identify and exploit zero-day vulnerabilities and could make attacks faster and more frequent; banks were asked to review cyber defences, third-party resilience, and incident response and recovery.

    Fuente: Hong Kong Monetary Authority

  • Aviso22 may 2026Financial Services Agency of Japan and Bank of Japan · ReguladorJapón

    Japan FSA and Bank of Japan request short-term measures against frontier AI threats

    The FSA and the Bank of Japan jointly asked financial institutions to take short-term responses to changing security threats from frontier AI models.

    Fuente: Financial Services Agency of Japan

  • Aviso21 may 2026New York State Department of Financial Services · ReguladorEstados Unidos

    NYDFS warns regulated entities of heightened cybersecurity risks from frontier AI models

    An industry letter to CISOs said frontier AI models could make finding and exploiting vulnerabilities faster and more scalable, and recommended accelerated vulnerability management, third-party coordination, human oversight of AI-generated code and enhanced monitoring.

    Fuente: New York State Department of Financial Services

  • Aviso18 may 2026Japan National Cybersecurity Office (Cabinet Secretariat) with FSA, MIC, MHLW, METI, MLIT, MOD, NPA and Cabinet Office · Agencia de seguridadJapón

    Japan alerts critical infrastructure operators to misuse of high-performance AI (Project YATA-Shield)

    A joint alert said AI misuse could sharply increase the speed and scale of cyber attacks, citing frontier models' vulnerability-discovery capabilities, and asked critical infrastructure operators to act under executive leadership, reinforce basics and speed vulnerability fixes.

    Fuente: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare of Japan

  • Aviso15 may 2026Bank of England, Financial Conduct Authority and HM Treasury · ReguladorReino Unido

    UK financial authorities issue joint statement on frontier AI models and cyber resilience

    The authorities said frontier AI models can exceed skilled human attackers in speed, scale and cost, and set expectations for firms and FMIs on governance, vulnerability management, third-party risk, protection, and response and recovery. No new rules were introduced.

    Fuente: Bank of England

  • Aviso30 abr 2026Australian Prudential Regulation Authority · ReguladorAustralia

    APRA letter calls for step-change in AI risk management at banks, insurers and super funds

    APRA said AI governance and risk management lag adoption, and warned that frontier AI models could help malicious actors find vulnerabilities, raising the likelihood, speed and scale of cyber attacks. It did not introduce new requirements.

    Fuente: Australian Prudential Regulation Authority

  • Alerta28 abr 2026Europol · IntergubernamentalEU

    Europol IOCTA 2026 says AI, encryption and proxies are expanding cybercrime

    Europol's Internet Organised Crime Threat Assessment said generative AI helps fraudsters produce more convincing, tailored scams and anticipated that self-directed AI could take over parts of illicit activity.

    Fuente: Europol
