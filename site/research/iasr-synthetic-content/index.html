AI-made fakes fuel fraud and extortion

Seen in real incidents81 matching case files

ShareLinkedInXFacebookWhatsAppTelegramEmail

The warning

Realistic AI-generated voices, images and video are used for fraud, extortion and sexual imagery made without consent, and cheap tools have made such content easy to produce at scale.

Paraphrased from the source. Source: International AI Safety Report 2026, Feb 3, 2026, section 2.1.1, page 45.

What the registry shows

The largest category in the registry: cloned voices and deepfake video calls impersonating executives, officials and relatives, and deepfake celebrity ads behind investment scams. The report adds that victims often do not report, so public cases undercount the harm.

Verified case files show this risk happening in the real world. First real-world case on file: Jan 5, 2025 · Already on file when this research was published.
