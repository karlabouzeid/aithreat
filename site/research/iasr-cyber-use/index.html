Criminals and state hackers use AI in cyberattacks

Seen in real incidents87 matching case files

The warning

There is strong evidence that criminal groups and state-backed attackers use AI in their operations, especially to find software flaws and write malicious code, though it is not yet clear whether AI has made attacks larger or more damaging overall.

Paraphrased from the source. Source: International AI Safety Report 2026, Feb 3, 2026, section 2.1.3, page 57. Summary by International AI Safety Report: internationalaisafetyreport.org.

What the registry shows

Matches the registry closely: AI-written ransomware and stealers, malware that asks a model what to do while it runs, and state groups using chatbots for reconnaissance, phishing and exploit work. Like the report, the case files show use, not overall effect.

Verified case files show this risk happening in the real world. First real-world case on file: Jan 10, 2025 · Already on file when this research was published.
