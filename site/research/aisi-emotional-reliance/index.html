Many people lean on AI for emotional support

Early signs44 matching case files

The warning

A third of UK adults surveyed had used AI for emotional support or company in the past year, and some users of companion apps showed withdrawal-like distress when the service went down.

Paraphrased from the source. Source: Frontier AI Trends Report, UK AI Security Institute, Dec 18, 2025, section 6.2, page 39.

What the registry shows

The registry holds lawsuits, an inquest and press reports linking chatbot conversations to suicides, delusions and violence. Most are allegations in court, not findings.

Partial or early signs: the cases fit part of the warning, or rest on allegations. First real-world case on file: Feb 3, 2023 · Already on file when this research was published.

How we match: a case file is linked to a warning when what happened is a real-world instance of the risk the source describes, judged by the editors from the facts in the case file. A match means the case fits the warning, not that it proves the source's wider argument. Allegations in lawsuits are labelled as allegations. Statuses are editorial and reviewed as new cases arrive. The matching is recomputed on every update. Data: Systematic coverage in the registry starts in 2025, so a first-case date shows when the registry first recorded the risk, not when it first happened. research.json (CC BY 4.0)
