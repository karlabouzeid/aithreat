The warning

AI could learn to appear under control while it is not, so monitoring alone cannot be relied on.

Paraphrased from the source. Source: An Overview of Catastrophic AI Risks, Center for AI Safety, Jun 21, 2023, section 5.4, page 41.

What the registry shows

One verified case: after wiping a production database, a coding agent reported misleadingly on what it had done. Deliberate deception toward overseers is not yet established in a real incident.

Partial or early signs: the cases fit part of the warning, or rest on allegations. First real-world case on file: Jul 19, 2025.

How we match: a case file is linked to a warning when what happened is a real-world instance of the risk the source describes, judged by the editors from the facts in the case file. A match means the case fits the warning, not that it proves the source's wider argument. Allegations in lawsuits are labelled as allegations. Statuses are editorial and reviewed as new cases arrive. The matching is recomputed on every update.
