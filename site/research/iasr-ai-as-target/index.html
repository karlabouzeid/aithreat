AI systems themselves become targets

Seen in real incidents85 matching case files

The warning

Attackers can manipulate AI systems through prompt injection, poisoned data and compromised components, to change how they behave or to pull out sensitive information.

Paraphrased from the source. Source: International AI Safety Report 2026, Feb 3, 2026, section 2.1.3, Box 2.1, page 62. Summary by International AI Safety Report: internationalaisafetyreport.org.

What the registry shows

Hidden instructions that make assistants leak mail and files, hijacked coding agents, malicious packages and plugins aimed at AI developers, and actively exploited flaws in AI tools. Many prompt injection cases are demonstrations by researchers; most supply-chain attacks happened in the real world.

Verified case files show this risk happening in the real world. First real-world case on file: Dec 30, 2022 · Already on file when this research was published.
