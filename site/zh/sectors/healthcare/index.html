  • 规则2026年6月2日Donald Trump, President of the United States (Executive Order 14409) · 政府首脑美国

    US executive order on advanced AI innovation and security addresses AI-enabled cyber threats

    The order directs agencies to prioritise cyber defence of government systems, has Treasury set up a voluntary AI cybersecurity clearinghouse, names rural hospitals, community banks and local utilities, and creates a voluntary pre-release framework for frontier models.

    来源: The White House

  • 指引2026年5月27日Japan Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (Health Policy Bureau) · 监管机构日本

    Japan health ministry alerts medical institutions to cyber risks from misuse of high-performance AI

    A notice to prefectures for medical institutions said AI misuse could sharply increase the speed and scale of cyber attacks, citing frontier models, and set priority measures on governance, vulnerability handling, ransomware, incident response and supply chains.

    来源: Ota City, Tokyo (republished MHLW notice)

  • 警告2026年5月22日Japan Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare (Minister Ueno) · 部长日本

    Japan health minister meets medical organisations on AI-driven cyberattacks

    At a meeting with medical organisations and the National Cybersecurity Office, the health minister said high-performance AI is increasing the threat of cyberattacks and that cybersecurity is a priority as healthcare digitalisation advances.

    来源: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare of Japan

  • 指引2026年5月18日Japan National Cybersecurity Office (Cabinet Secretariat) with FSA, MIC, MHLW, METI, MLIT, MOD, NPA and Cabinet Office · 安全机构日本

    Japan alerts critical infrastructure operators to misuse of high-performance AI (Project YATA-Shield)

    A joint alert said AI misuse could sharply increase the speed and scale of cyber attacks, citing frontier models' vulnerability-discovery capabilities, and asked critical infrastructure operators to act under executive leadership, reinforce basics and speed vulnerability fixes.

    来源: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare of Japan
