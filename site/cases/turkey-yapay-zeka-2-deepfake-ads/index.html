What happened

Osmaniye-centred raids in nine provinces detained 16 suspects, 11 jailed, for fake investment ads reproducing voices and footage of senior Turkish officials with AI; Istanbul later warned of ads using Imamoglu's image.

Impact: Losses reported only as hundreds of thousands of lira in some coverage; total not published

Who triggered it

Actor: Turkish investment-ad fraud network

Attribution: Turkish police operation in Osmaniye, Adana, Balikesir, Istanbul, Izmir, Antalya, Afyonkarahisar, Yalova and Tekirdag

Motive: Financial gain from fake investment schemes promising high returns

How it happened

Attack vector: AI-cloned voices and video of state officials in sponsored social media ads directing victims to fake investment schedules

Root cause: Generative audio and video tools plus paid social advertising; victims trusted apparent official endorsements

Timeline

  1. May 24, 2026Sixteen suspects detained in nine provinces; 11 jailed after court processing
  2. Jun 10, 2026Istanbul municipality warns about fake ads using Imamoglu's image

Response

Detentions and judicial arrests; municipal and media warnings

Current status: Under investigation
