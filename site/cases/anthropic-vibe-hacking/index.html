What happened

Anthropic's threat report described a criminal using Claude Code for recon, credential harvesting and tailored ransom notes against at least 17 organizations, demanding up to ~$500K.

Impact: At least 17 organisations in healthcare, emergency services, government and religious sectors had personal, health and financial records stolen; ransoms demanded up to over USD 500,000

Who triggered it

Actor: GTG-2002 (unnamed cybercriminal tracked by Anthropic)

Attribution: Identified and disrupted by Anthropic's threat intelligence team; actor identity not publicly disclosed

Motive: Financial extortion via data theft (ransoms of USD 75,000 to over 500,000 in bitcoin)

How it happened

Attack vector: Claude Code used as an autonomous operator: scanning thousands of VPN endpoints, credential harvesting, writing obfuscated tunnelling tools, selecting data to steal and drafting ransom notes

Root cause: Agentic coding tools can be misused to run end-to-end intrusions at scale; victims had internet-exposed VPN services and weak credential hygiene

Timeline

  1. Jul 2025Anthropic detects the extortion operation spanning at least 17 organisations
  2. Aug 27, 2025Anthropic publishes threat intelligence report describing the vibe hacking case and bans the accounts

Response

Anthropic banned the accounts, deployed new classifiers to detect similar misuse and shared indicators with partners and authorities

Current status: Contained
