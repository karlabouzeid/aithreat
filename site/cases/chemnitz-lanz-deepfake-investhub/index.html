What happened

Chemnitz police are investigating after a Saxony woman transferred sums from 250 to 460,000 euros to the Investhub 3.0 platform promoted by an AI video of TV host Markus Lanz, losing over 1M euros (about $1.1M).

Impact: Single victim lost over 1M euros across several transfers.

Who triggered it

Actor: Unknown

Attribution: Unknown.

Motive: Financial gain via fake trading platform.

How it happened

Attack vector: Deepfake video circulating since April 2026 showing Markus Lanz endorsing Investhub 3.0 with unusually high returns; victim made multiple transfers.

Root cause: Highly realistic AI video and audio of a trusted broadcaster used to lend credibility to an unlicensed platform.

Timeline

  1. Apr 1, 2026Deepfake Lanz video promoting Investhub 3.0 begins circulating
  2. Jul 4, 2026Victim files police report in Chemnitz
  3. Jul 8, 2026Case reported alongside new BaFin warnings

Response

Chemnitz police investigating; BaFin issued parallel warnings about identity-cloning investment sites.

Current status: Under investigation
