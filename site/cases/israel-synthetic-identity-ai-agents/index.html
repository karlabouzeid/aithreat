What happened

Israel Police allege a 20-year-old bought ID photos on the darknet, used AI to animate them past remote video account-opening checks, and used AI agents to open bank accounts and cards in hundreds of victims' names.

Impact: Hundreds of identities stolen; estimated theft of hundreds of thousands of shekels; victims unaware of accounts opened in their names.

Who triggered it

Actor: 20-year-old suspect from Tzur Yitzhak

Attribution: Police allegations; suspect's lawyer says the investigation is preliminary.

Motive: Cash withdrawals and purchases on fraudulently obtained cards.

How it happened

Attack vector: AI-animated synthetic identities defeated liveness and selfie checks; AI agents automated account and card applications; AI tools used to hide the suspect's identity.

Root cause: Remote KYC relying on live video and selfies that generative AI can now satisfy.

Timeline

  1. Jun 18, 2026Rishon LeZion court extends suspect's detention by a week
  2. Jun 21, 2026Calcalist reports police allegations; about 90 warrants and 120 complainants

Response

Arrest, detention extension and ongoing Lahav 433 investigation.

Current status: Under investigation
