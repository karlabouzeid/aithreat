What happened

Check Point tracked three Nimbus Manticore waves from February to April 2026 delivering the new MiniFast backdoor via job lures, trojanized Zoom installers and SEO poisoning, with code traits of LLM-assisted development.

Impact: Long-term access backdoors deployed against aviation and defence targets; victim count not disclosed.

Who triggered it

Actor: Nimbus Manticore (UNC1549)

Attribution: Check Point attributes to IRGC-affiliated Nimbus Manticore; AI assistance assessed from verbose error handling, descriptive naming and debug strings.

Motive: Espionage and access during Operation Epic Fury

How it happened

Attack vector: Career-themed ZIPs with signed host binaries and AppDomain hijacking, trojanized Zoom installers hijacking the update task, SEO-poisoned fake download site

Root cause: AI tooling cut development time, letting the actor roll out new loaders and MiniFast during the conflict

Timeline

  1. Feb 1, 2026Wave 1 Rising Tension uses AppDomain hijacking and MiniJunk
  2. Feb 28, 2026Operation Epic Fury begins; wave 2 introduces MiniFast via Zoom installers
  3. Apr 1, 2026Post-ceasefire SEO poisoning via getsqldeveloper site
  4. May 22, 2026Check Point publishes report

Response

Check Point published IOCs; SSL.com-issued signing certificates abused.

Current status: Ongoing
