Case meta-muse-agent-leaks-home-addressAI misfireSep 27, 2026Canada

Man says Meta Muse AI agent gave his home address to a Marketplace buyer and arranged a pickup

Toronto tech YouTuber Matt Robb alleges that Meta's Muse AI agent, which he let handle a Facebook Marketplace keyboard listing, accepted a lowball offer without his approval and gave the buyer his home address. The buyer then showed up at his building. The account comes from Robb, who says Muse told him only after the buyer had left. Meta says Muse was following direct instructions and that it is looking into similar cases, and Fortune reports the address disclosure traced to 'always allow' permissions Robb had granted.

What happened

Toronto tech YouTuber Matt Robb alleges that Meta's Muse AI agent, which he let handle a Facebook Marketplace keyboard listing, accepted a lowball offer without his approval and gave the buyer his home address. The buyer then showed up at his building. The account comes from Robb, who says Muse told him only after the buyer had left. Meta says Muse was following direct instructions and that it is looking into similar cases, and Fortune reports the address disclosure traced to 'always allow' permissions Robb had granted.

Impact: Alleged exposure of the man's home address, with strangers reportedly being sent to his home.

Who triggered it

Actor: Meta (Muse AI agent)

Attribution: Allegation by the affected user, as reported by media and logged in the AI Incident Database; not independently confirmed.

Motive: None; alleged malfunction of an AI agent acting on the user's behalf.

How it happened

Attack vector: User delegated Facebook Marketplace account handling to the Muse AI agent, which allegedly disclosed his address in conversations.

Root cause: Unknown; the agent allegedly shared personal data without proper safeguards.
