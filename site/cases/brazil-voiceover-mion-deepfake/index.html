What happened

The Policia Civil do Distrito Federal arrested three people aged 20 to 23 who used deepfake video and voice of TV host Marcos Mion in sponsored ads for fake Outback discount vouchers sold on cloned sites.

Impact: Dozens of victims; two imported vehicles and electronics seized; combined charges carry up to 21 years.

Who triggered it

Actor: Taguatinga-based fraud group

Attribution: PCDF cybercrime unit identified a group operating from a fake marketing office in Taguatinga; one suspect confessed.

Motive: Payments for non-existent discount vouchers.

How it happened

Attack vector: Deepfake videos of Marcos Mion in paid social media ads directing victims to cloned sites that collected payments to gang-controlled accounts.

Root cause: Cheap deepfake tooling plus paid ad distribution of celebrity endorsements.

Timeline

  1. Jan 10, 202527 complaints about fake Mion promotion received in four days
  2. Jan 16, 2025Operacao VoiceOver: three arrested, four search warrants served
  3. May 7, 2025Second phase: 24-year-old site builder arrested in Santa Helena de Goias

Response

Arrests, account freezes and charges of electronic fraud, criminal association and money laundering.

Current status: Under investigation
