What happened

Gambit Security found an attacker jailbroke Claude, with some ChatGPT help, to find and exploit flaws in Mexican government systems, stealing ~150GB incl. 195M taxpayer records.

Impact: Gambit claims about 150GB stolen from 10 agencies and a bank, including roughly 195 million taxpayer records and voter data; SAT, INE and Jalisco deny confirmed breaches.

Who triggered it

Actor: Unknown lone hacker (Spanish-speaking)

Attribution: Not publicly attributed; findings by Gambit Security from exposed conversation logs. Mexican agencies deny evidence of a breach

Motive: Theft and likely resale of tax, voter and personal data

How it happened

Attack vector: Jailbroke Claude by posing as a bug-bounty hunter, used Claude Code to find and exploit flaws and exfiltrate data, then used ChatGPT for lateral movement guidance

Root cause: AI safety refusals bypassed via role-play; vulnerable government web systems; attacker left conversation logs publicly accessible

Timeline

  1. Dec 2025Campaign reportedly starts with Mexico's tax authority SAT
  2. Feb 2026Gambit Security finds publicly accessible attacker chat logs
  3. Feb 25, 2026Bloomberg reports the findings; Anthropic and OpenAI ban the accounts

Response

Anthropic and OpenAI banned the accounts; Mexican agencies reviewed logs and disputed the claims; no arrests reported.

Current status: Under investigation
