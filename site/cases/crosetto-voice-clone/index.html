AI-cloned voice of Italy's defence minister used to con business leaders

Scammers mimicked Defence Minister Guido Crosetto's voice to solicit ransom money for 'kidnapped journalists'; Massimo Moratti wired nearly EUR 1M, which police later froze in the Netherlands.

What happened

Scammers mimicked Defence Minister Guido Crosetto's voice to solicit ransom money for 'kidnapped journalists'; Massimo Moratti wired nearly EUR 1M, which police later froze in the Netherlands.

Impact: Massimo Moratti wired almost EUR 1 million; Giorgio Armani, Patrizio Bertelli and other elites targeted but did not pay; funds later frozen

Who triggered it

Actor: Unknown fraud ring; two Dutch nationals placed under investigation

Attribution: Milan prosecutors placed two foreign (Dutch) nationals under investigation per ANSA; believed to be acting for others; no public arrests confirmed

Motive: Financial fraud (fake ransom for kidnapped journalists)

How it happened

Attack vector: Phone calls from fake defence ministry officials handing over to an AI-cloned voice of Minister Crosetto requesting urgent wire transfers to foreign accounts

Root cause: Realistic voice cloning of a public figure combined with urgency and authority pretext; victims had no out-of-band verification before wiring funds

Timeline

  1. Feb 2025Callers posing as Crosetto contact Armani, Bertelli, Moratti and other business leaders seeking ransom money
  2. Feb 2025Moratti wires two payments totalling almost EUR 1M, then files a complaint with Milan prosecutors
  3. Feb 12, 2025Italian police announce the funds were traced to a Dutch account and completely frozen
  4. Feb 13, 2025ANSA reports two Dutch nationals under investigation; Milan prosecutors probe organised-crime group formation

Response

Moratti filed a complaint; Milan prosecutors opened an organised-crime probe; funds traced and frozen in the Netherlands; two suspects identified

Current status: Under investigation
