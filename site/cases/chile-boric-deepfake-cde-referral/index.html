What happened

Security Minister Luis Cordero said the government was weighing a referral to the Consejo de Defensa del Estado over a deepfake that spliced Boric's voice and image into an ad for a supposed investment platform.

Impact: Presidential likeness repeatedly used in scams; victim losses not quantified.

Who triggered it

Actor: Unknown

Attribution: No attribution; reporting linked earlier Boric and Codelco fakes to campaigns run from Uruguay for XMR Markets.

Motive: Investment fraud.

How it happened

Attack vector: AI-edited presidential footage in paid social media ads promising returns of 8 million pesos on 200,000.

Root cause: No legal framework for deepfakes in Chile at the time; ad platforms slow to act.

Timeline

  1. Jun 4, 2025Study reports Boric lithium-investment deepfake among top social scams

Response

Ministerial denunciation and possible CDE action; legislative response in 2026.
