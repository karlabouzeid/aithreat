What happened

Attackers who compromised the Context.ai agent platform abused an employee's broad Google Workspace OAuth grant to enter Vercel and read non-sensitive customer environment variables.

Impact: Attacker accessed some Vercel environments and customer environment variables not marked sensitive for a limited set of customers; sensitive-flagged variables showed no evidence of access

Who triggered it

Actor: Unknown threat actor; data-sale claims on BreachForums unverified

Attribution: No public attribution; Vercel self-disclosed and traced origin to Context.ai compromise with Google Mandiant

Motive: Credential and data theft; possible resale (unverified extortion claims)

How it happened

Attack vector: Compromised Context.ai Google Workspace OAuth application used to take over a Vercel employee's corporate Google account, then pivot into Vercel environments

Root cause: Broad OAuth scopes granted to a third-party AI tool, and Vercel environment variables not marked sensitive being readable in plaintext

Timeline

  1. Feb 2026Context.ai employee reportedly infected with Lumma Stealer; OAuth tokens later compromised
  2. Mar 2026Context.ai discloses unauthorized access to its AWS environment and hires CrowdStrike
  3. Apr 19, 2026Vercel detects unauthorized access to internal systems via employee Google Workspace account
  4. Apr 21, 2026Vercel publishes bulletin, notifies affected customers and publishes OAuth app IOC

Response

Vercel engaged Google Mandiant, notified affected customers, recommended credential rotation, published an OAuth app IOC and confirmed with GitHub, npm and Socket that its packages were untouched

Current status: Under investigation
