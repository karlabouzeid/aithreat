Alertes officielles et préparation

Alertes publiques, exercices, avis et nouvelles règles émanant de chefs de gouvernement, ministres, régulateurs, banques centrales, organisations intergouvernementales et agences nationales de cybersécurité. Chaque entrée renvoie à sa source.

  • Avis16 oct. 2026FBI and CISA · Agence de sécuritéÉtats-Unis

    FBI and CISA warn election officials of AI-assisted phishing and deepfakes

    A joint PSA said cyber actors targeting election and campaign officials use AI to write natural-sounding phishing emails and create deepfake video and audio, and advised checking content credentials or watermarks.

    Source: FBI IC3

  • Avis15 oct. 2026FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center · Agence de sécuritéÉtats-Unis

    FBI warns scammers exploit 2026 midterms with AI impersonation of candidates

    The FBI warned of fake PACs, candidate investment pools, bogus merchandise and fake voter registration, noting scammers use AI to create realistic voices, images and messages impersonating candidates and campaigns.

    Source: FBI IC3

  • Alerte22 sept. 2026ENISA · IntergouvernementalEU

    ENISA Threat Landscape 2026 reports growing malicious use of AI by threat groups

    ENISA said threat groups increasingly use AI and also target AI systems, that synthetic audio, video and text are part of influence operators' daily arsenal, and that AI integration extends the attack surface.

    Source: ENISA

  • Alerte23 juin 2026European Parliament Special Committee on the European Democracy Shield · ParlementEU

    European Parliament committee calls for stronger measures against malicious deepfakes

    The special committee adopted a report calling for stronger measures against malicious deepfakes and fraudulent ads, exposure of hidden generative AI disinformation campaigns and faster platform responses to bot-driven interference.

    Source: European Parliamentary Research Service

  • Avis14 mai 2026Singapore Police Force · Agence de sécuritéSingapour

    Singapore police warn of deepfake Zoom scam impersonating senior government officials

    Police said a victim lost at least S$4.9 million after a Zoom call whose participants, appearing to include the Prime Minister, were fabricated with deepfake AI, and warned that officials never ask for money transfers.

    Source: Singapore Police Force

  • Règle19 avr. 2026Election Commission of India · RégulateurInde

    Election Commission of India requires 3-hour action on unlawful AI content during state polls

    The ECI directed that misleading or unlawful AI-generated content be acted on within 3 hours of notice to platforms, and that parties label synthetic campaign material and disclose its origin. Over 11,000 posts were acted on since 15 March.

    Source: Press Information Bureau, Government of India

  • Exercice16 avr. 2026UK Electoral Commission · RégulateurRoyaume-Uni

    UK Electoral Commission pilots deepfake detection for May 2026 elections

    The Commission launched a pilot with the Home Office's Accelerated Capability Environment to detect political deepfakes during the May elections in England, Scotland and Wales, with powers to refer cases to police and ask platforms to remove content.

    Source: Local Government Lawyer

  • Alerte8 mars 2026National Election Commission of Korea · RégulateurCorée du Sud

    South Korea's election commission and police to step up deepfake crackdown before local elections

    The National Election Commission and police said they would strengthen crackdowns on deepfakes and misinformation ahead of the 3 June 2026 local elections, under a law banning election deepfakes in the 90 days before a vote.

    Source: The Korea Herald

  • Règle2 mars 2026Superior Electoral Court (TSE) · RégulateurBrésil

    Brazil's electoral court adopts AI rules for 2026 general elections

    The TSE approved resolutions requiring disclosure of synthetic campaign content, barring new AI-generated content from 72 hours before to 24 hours after the vote, and prohibiting AI chatbots from recommending or ranking candidates.

    Source: AFP via Citizen Digital

  • Avis17 déc. 2025Viginum (SGDSN) · Agence de sécuritéFrance

    French agency Viginum issues guide warning municipal candidates of information manipulation risks

    Ahead of the March 2026 municipal elections, Viginum published an awareness guide for campaign teams with documented cases, including a Storm-1516 deepfake video, and advised monitoring, crisis exercises and reporting deepfakes to Arcom.

    Source: Banque des Territoires (Localtis)

  • Alerte25 nov. 2025European Parliament · ParlementEU

    European Parliament flags unlabelled AI deepfake videos in Hungary ahead of 2026 elections

    In its Hungary rule-of-law report, Parliament voiced concern about unlabelled AI-generated political content, including deepfake videos on channels linked to the governing party, saying such use could mislead voters and threaten the fairness of elections.

    Source: European Parliament
