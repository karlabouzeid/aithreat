    US agencies warn of industrial-scale knowledge distillation campaigns against US AI models

    Source: CISA

  • Avis28 juil. 2026eSafety Commissioner (Australia) · RégulateurAustralie

    eSafety urged schools to review how they share images online after receiving over 100 reports in January to March 2026 of accounts misusing school photos, including AI-generated sexualised deepfakes and face swaps of students and staff.

    Source: Human Rights Watch

  • Règle7 juil. 2026UK Department for Education · MinistreRoyaume-Uni

    Keeping Children Safe in Education 2026 covers AI-generated nude images

    The 2026 statutory safeguarding guidance, in force from 1 September 2026, defines sharing nudes and semi-nudes to include images digitally altered or wholly generated using AI, and links schools to generative AI safety expectations.

    Source: EdTech Innovation Hub

  • Alerte8 mai 2026UK National Crime Agency and Early Warning Working Group · Agence de sécuritéRoyaume-Uni

    UK schools advised to remove identifiable pupil photos as AI blackmail threat grows

    The National Crime Agency warned that criminals use AI to alter children's photos taken from school sites and demand payment, and schools were advised to remove or limit identifiable pupil images online.

    Source: Internet Watch Foundation (republishing The Guardian)

  • Alerte22 avr. 2026White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (Director Michael Kratsios) · Chef de gouvernementÉtats-Unis

    White House memo NSTM-4 addresses adversarial distillation of US AI models

    The memo said foreign entities, principally in China, run industrial-scale campaigns to distil US frontier AI models, and committed agencies to share information with AI developers and explore accountability measures.

    Source: The White House

  • Avis15 avr. 2026Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (Healey-Driscoll administration) · RégulateurÉtats-Unis

    Massachusetts tells school districts to promptly investigate AI deepfake images of students

    A letter to districts statewide said creating a sexual deepfake of a minor is a crime and directed school leaders to investigate complaints about AI-generated explicit images of students promptly.

    Source: The Boston Globe

  • Avis19 janv. 2026UK Department for Education · MinistreRoyaume-Uni

    UK Department for Education updates generative AI product safety standards for schools

    The update added standards on cognitive, emotional and social development, mental health and protection against manipulation to the capabilities generative AI products used in schools and colleges should meet.

    Source: GOV.UK
