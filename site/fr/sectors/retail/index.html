Alertes publiques, exercices, avis et nouvelles règles émanant de chefs de gouvernement, ministres, régulateurs, banques centrales, organisations intergouvernementales et agences nationales de cybersécurité. Chaque entrée renvoie à sa source.

  • Avis15 oct. 2026FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center · Agence de sécuritéÉtats-Unis

    FBI warns scammers exploit 2026 midterms with AI impersonation of candidates

    The FBI warned of fake PACs, candidate investment pools, bogus merchandise and fake voter registration, noting scammers use AI to create realistic voices, images and messages impersonating candidates and campaigns.

    Source: FBI IC3

  • Avis17 sept. 2026FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center · Agence de sécuritéÉtats-Unis

    FBI warns of law enforcement and government impersonation scams using AI on video calls

    The FBI said scammers use AI to appear as officials on video calls, and reported nearly 61,000 government-impersonation complaints with losses over USD 1.6 billion between January 2025 and July 2026.

    Source: FBI IC3

  • Alerte17 août 2026Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC) · RégulateurAustralie

    ASIC warns scammers use AI to build networks of deepfake websites and endorsements

    ASIC said deepfake videos of celebrities and politicians in investment scams rose sharply, removed over 19,400 scams in FY26 (up 182%), and advised that a simple online search is not enough to verify an offer.

    Source: ASIC

  • Alerte3 août 2026INTERPOL · IntergouvernementalINT

    INTERPOL report finds AI involved in 55% of reported cybercrime in Africa

    INTERPOL's African Cyberthreat Assessment 2026 said AI is involved in 55% of reported cybercrimes, losses more than doubled to USD 484 million since 2024, and deepfakes and synthetic identities fuel sextortion and fraud.

    Source: INTERPOL

  • Avis20 juil. 2026FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center · Agence de sécuritéÉtats-Unis

    FBI warns scammers impersonate IC3 using deepfake videos of public figures

    The FBI said recovery scammers use AI-generated deepfake videos of public figures, including a senior FBI leader, to promote fake IC3 complaint sites that harvest personal and financial data from past fraud victims.

    Source: FBI IC3

  • Avis24 juin 2026Federal Trade Commission · RégulateurÉtats-Unis

    FTC consumer alert warns pet scams use AI-generated images and deepfakes

    The FTC warned that scammers use AI-generated deepfakes and images of pets in fake emergency and donation schemes, and advised reverse image searches and avoiding gift card, crypto or wire payments.

    Source: Federal Trade Commission

  • Alerte15 juin 2026Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA) · RégulateurAfrique du Sud

    South Africa's FSCA warns of deepfake videos of journalists promoting investment scam

    The FSCA warned that AI-generated videos of journalist Maya Fisher-French and broadcaster Bruce Whitfield were used in social media ads promoting a 'Quantum AI' scheme promising unrealistic returns.

    Source: Eyewitness News

  • Avis14 mai 2026Singapore Police Force · Agence de sécuritéSingapour

    Singapore police warn of deepfake Zoom scam impersonating senior government officials

    Police said a victim lost at least S$4.9 million after a Zoom call whose participants, appearing to include the Prime Minister, were fabricated with deepfake AI, and warned that officials never ask for money transfers.

    Source: Singapore Police Force

  • Alerte28 avr. 2026Europol · IntergouvernementalEU

    Europol IOCTA 2026 says AI, encryption and proxies are expanding cybercrime

    Europol's Internet Organised Crime Threat Assessment said generative AI makes fraud more convincing, ransomware groups are adopting AI tools, and it expects cybercrime to become more automated with minimal human involvement.

    Source: eucrim

  • Alerte13 avr. 2026Federal Office for Information Security (BSI) and Police Crime Prevention (ProPK) · Agence de sécuritéAllemagne

    German BSI survey finds few internet users check AI-generated content as AI investment scams spread

    BSI and police found only 19% of users check a source's credibility and 28% look for image inconsistencies, and warned of investment fraud using AI-made videos of celebrities.

    Source: BSI

  • Alerte16 mars 2026INTERPOL · IntergouvernementalINT

    INTERPOL fraud assessment warns AI is industrialising global financial fraud

    INTERPOL's Global Financial Fraud Threat Assessment said AI-enhanced fraud is 4.5 times more profitable than traditional methods and that agentic AI tools can run entire fraud campaigns, from scouting to extortion.

    Source: INTERPOL
