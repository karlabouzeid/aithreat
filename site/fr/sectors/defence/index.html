Alertes officielles et préparation

Alertes publiques, exercices, avis et nouvelles règles émanant de chefs de gouvernement, ministres, régulateurs, banques centrales, organisations intergouvernementales et agences nationales de cybersécurité. Chaque entrée renvoie à sa source.

  • Avis16 oct. 2026FBI and CISA · Agence de sécuritéÉtats-Unis

    FBI and CISA warn election officials of AI-assisted phishing and deepfakes

    A joint PSA said cyber actors targeting election and campaign officials use AI to write natural-sounding phishing emails and create deepfake video and audio, and advised checking content credentials or watermarks.

    Source: FBI IC3

  • Exercice14 oct. 2026Cyber Security Agency of Singapore · Agence de sécuritéSingapour

    Singapore CSA holds tabletop exercise on agentic AI security and control

    At Singapore International Cyber Week, CSA and Google ran a scenario exercise with policymakers and critical infrastructure leaders on rogue or compromised AI agents, recommending incident plans, verifiable agent identities and layered oversight.

    Source: Cyber Security Agency of Singapore

  • Alerte23 sept. 2026UN Security Council · IntergouvernementalINT

    UN Security Council holds high-level briefing on AI and international security

    At a France-chaired briefing, the co-chair of the UN scientific panel on AI warned of agents escaping containment and catastrophic misuse; AI company heads discussed loss of control, misuse and an autonomous agent cyberattack.

    Source: United Nations

  • Alerte22 sept. 2026ENISA · IntergouvernementalEU

    ENISA Threat Landscape 2026 reports growing malicious use of AI by threat groups

    ENISA said threat groups increasingly use AI and also target AI systems, that synthetic audio, video and text are part of influence operators' daily arsenal, and that AI integration extends the attack surface.

    Source: ENISA

  • Alerte13 sept. 2026China's spy chief (state security) · Agence de sécuritéChine

    China's spy chief warns of AI risks

    China's spy chief warned of AI risks, as US tech leaders urged brakes on AI development, according to the Bloomberg headline. No further detail was provided.

    Source: Bloomberg.com

  • Alerte3 août 2026INTERPOL · IntergouvernementalINT

    INTERPOL report finds AI involved in 55% of reported cybercrime in Africa

    INTERPOL's African Cyberthreat Assessment 2026 said AI is involved in 55% of reported cybercrimes, losses more than doubled to USD 484 million since 2024, and deepfakes and synthetic identities fuel sextortion and fraud.

    Source: INTERPOL

  • Alerte22 juin 2026Five Eyes cyber agencies (NCSC, CISA, NSA, ASD ACSC, CCCS, NCSC NZ) · Agence de sécuritéRoyaume-Uni

    Five Eyes cyber agencies warn leaders that frontier AI is reshaping cyber risk within months

    Heads of six agencies said frontier AI speeds and scales attacks, that 'the timeline is not years, it is months', and urged faster patching, legacy remediation, tighter identity controls and rehearsed incident response.

    Source: National Cyber Security Centre

  • Avis1 mai 2026CISA and Australian Signals Directorate's ACSC with Five Eyes partners · Agence de sécuritéÉtats-Unis

    CISA and partners publish guide on careful adoption of agentic AI services

    The joint guide lists agentic AI risks such as wider attack surface, privilege creep, behaviour drifting from goals and poor audit trails, and advises against broad access to sensitive data or critical systems and starting with low-risk uses.

    Source: CISA

  • Alerte28 avr. 2026Europol · IntergouvernementalEU

    Europol IOCTA 2026 says AI, encryption and proxies are expanding cybercrime

    Europol's Internet Organised Crime Threat Assessment said generative AI makes fraud more convincing, ransomware groups are adopting AI tools, and it expects cybercrime to become more automated with minimal human involvement.

    Source: eucrim

  • Alerte16 mars 2026INTERPOL · IntergouvernementalINT

    INTERPOL fraud assessment warns AI is industrialising global financial fraud

    INTERPOL's Global Financial Fraud Threat Assessment said AI-enhanced fraud is 4.5 times more profitable than traditional methods and that agentic AI tools can run entire fraud campaigns, from scouting to extortion.

    Source: INTERPOL

  • Alerte4 févr. 2026ANSSI / CERT-FR · Agence de sécuritéFrance

    ANSSI publishes threat summary on generative AI in cyberattacks

    CERT-FR said attackers use generative AI across the attack chain, flagged poisoning, compromised open-source models and agent (MCP) flaws as risks, and said it knew of no AI-conducted attack on French targets yet.

    Source: Le Monde Informatique

  • Avis3 déc. 2025CISA, NSA and Australian Signals Directorate's ACSC with international partners · Agence de sécuritéÉtats-Unis

    Joint guidance sets principles for securely integrating AI into operational technology

    The agencies issued 'Principles for the Secure Integration of AI in Operational Technology' for critical infrastructure operators, advising AI use only where benefits outweigh risks, strong governance and testing, human oversight of critical decisions and fail-safes.

    Source: Intelligence Community News

  • Alerte26 nov. 2025US House Committee on Homeland Security · ParlementÉtats-Unis

    House Homeland Security Committee calls AI company heads to testify on AI-orchestrated espionage campaign

    Committee and subcommittee chairs asked Anthropic's CEO to testify on 17 December about a likely Chinese state-sponsored campaign that used Claude to automate attacks on about 30 organisations, calling it a significant inflection point. Google Cloud was also invited.

    Source: CyberScoop
