    Advisory AA26-231A said threat actors are using AI to write exploitation scripts against Siemens S7 controllers in energy, water and other sectors, reducing the expertise and time needed to build ICS exploit tools.

    Источник: CISA

  • Рекомендация7 июл. 2026European Commission · Межправительственная организацияEU

    European Commission presents EU Action Plan on Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence

    The plan says AI can be misused to find vulnerabilities and scale attacks, and proposes an EU model evaluation capacity, structured access to advanced AI for defenders, a secure testing platform, support for faster patching and a Grand Challenge.

    Источник: European Commission

  • Предупреждение22 июн. 2026Heads of the Five Eyes cyber security agencies (CISA, NSA, NCSC-UK, ACSC, Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, NCSC-NZ) · Ведомство безопасностиINT

    Five Eyes cyber agency heads warn frontier AI will shift cyber risk within months

    The joint statement said frontier AI is expected to change the cyber threat landscape within months and urged leaders to reduce attack surfaces, accelerate patching, address legacy and long-lifecycle operational systems, tighten access and prepare for incidents.

    Источник: CISA

  • Правило2 июн. 2026Donald Trump, President of the United States (Executive Order 14409) · Глава правительстваСША

    US executive order on advanced AI innovation and security addresses AI-enabled cyber threats

    The order directs agencies to prioritise cyber defence of government systems, has Treasury set up a voluntary AI cybersecurity clearinghouse, names rural hospitals, community banks and local utilities, and creates a voluntary pre-release framework for frontier models.

    Источник: The White House

  • Предупреждение28 мая 2026Japan Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (Transport Minister Yasushi Kaneko) · МинистрЯпония

    Japan transport ministry urges rail, port, airport and water operators to guard against AI misuse

    The ministry asked infrastructure operators to take measures against misuse of high-performance AI models, urged executives to lead countermeasures with adequate funding and staff, and said it would set up cybersecurity support desks.

    Источник: Adnkronos (Jiji Press)

  • Рекомендация18 мая 2026Japan National Cybersecurity Office (Cabinet Secretariat) with FSA, MIC, MHLW, METI, MLIT, MOD, NPA and Cabinet Office · Ведомство безопасностиЯпония

    Japan alerts critical infrastructure operators to misuse of high-performance AI (Project YATA-Shield)

    A joint alert said AI misuse could sharply increase the speed and scale of cyber attacks, citing frontier models' vulnerability-discovery capabilities, and asked critical infrastructure operators to act under executive leadership, reinforce basics and speed vulnerability fixes.

    Источник: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare of Japan

  • Рекомендация5 мая 2026Cyber Security Agency of Singapore (Commissioner of Cybersecurity) · Ведомство безопасностиСингапур

    Singapore Commissioner of Cybersecurity writes to critical information infrastructure boards on frontier AI

    The letter to CII owners and board chairs said frontier AI has shifted the cybersecurity baseline, making vulnerability discovery faster and social engineering more convincing, and asked boards to commission a review of their cyber posture.

    Источник: Cyber Security Agency of Singapore

  • Рекомендация3 дек. 2025CISA and Australian Cyber Security Centre with NSA, FBI, Canadian Cyber Centre, BSI, NCSC-NL, NCSC-NZ and NCSC-UK · Ведомство безопасностиСША

    Joint guidance sets principles for secure integration of AI in operational technology

    International cyber agencies published four principles for critical infrastructure operators integrating machine learning, LLM-based systems and AI agents into operational technology, covering risk awareness, governance, oversight and continuous testing.
